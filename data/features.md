# Suggested features: psychedelics site
- Interaction checker (substance x medication matrix, sourced, "seek medical advice" defaults). Show SSRI/MAOI/lithium warnings prominently.
- Legal/trial status timeline by country with a "last verified" date per row.
- Evidence-strength badges (RCT / observational / anecdotal) on every fact.
- Crisis and harm-reduction resource panel by country (e.g. FRANK, Fireside Project, poison centres).
- Historical/cultural context pages crediting Indigenous traditions (Mazatec, Bwiti, Amazonian, Native American Church).
- "What a clinical trial session looks like" explainer, based on Johnson 2008 guidelines.
- Myth vs evidence cards; a glossary; print-friendly safety sheets.
